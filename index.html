Fintrac Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Summary: Fintrac is designed to help users manage personal finance records. Automatic transaction-alert processing is optional and requires the user's explicit action and Android permission.

1. Who We Are

Fintrac is a personal finance companion application. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use its account, transaction-record, optional transaction-alert, and WhatsApp-assistant features.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Email address and account-authentication information.
  • Finance records you create or import, such as transaction amount, currency, date, debit or credit direction, merchant when available, bank sender, and assigned category.
  • Optional WhatsApp-linking and messaging information, such as your linked WhatsApp number and conversation state, when you choose to use the WhatsApp assistant.
  • App preferences and consent records, including whether optional transaction-alert import is enabled, paused, or withdrawn.

3. Optional Transaction-Alert Processing

Automatic transaction-alert processing is optional. It begins only after you select “Enable transaction alerts” in the app and approve Android's SMS permission prompt.

The app does not automatically import or scan earlier SMS history. It considers only future eligible bank transaction-alert messages received after activation.

Eligible matching messages are parsed on your device to identify structured finance information, such as transaction amount, date, debit/credit direction, bank sender, and merchant when available.

Full raw SMS message text and one-time passwords (OTPs) are not saved as transaction records. The feature is not used for SMS login verification, OTP interception, SMS forwarding, or unrelated message access.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store, display, organise, and categorise your finance records.
  • Prevent duplicate transaction records.
  • Provide optional transaction-alert processing that you enable.
  • Provide the optional WhatsApp assistant and related conversation flows, where you have linked and opted in to WhatsApp messaging.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

We use service providers to operate the app, including Supabase for account authentication and database storage. If you choose to use the WhatsApp assistant, relevant information needed to provide that service may be processed through the WhatsApp messaging integration.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not sell raw SMS content or OTPs.

6. Your Choices and Controls

You can:

  • Choose not to enable transaction-alert processing.
  • Pause transaction-alert processing inside the app.
  • Remove SMS permission in Android Settings at any time.
  • Choose whether to link and opt in to WhatsApp messaging.
  • Contact us to ask questions about your account or privacy.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain account information, finance records, consent records, and related operational data for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You may contact us regarding deletion requests.

9. Children's Privacy

Fintrac is not intended for children. Do not use the app if you are not legally permitted to use finance-related services in your location.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date.
